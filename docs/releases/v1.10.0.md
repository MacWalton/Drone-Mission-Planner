## New in 1.10: flight date and light

- **Flight date, site time zone and arrival time.** In QGIS they're in **When and conditions**; in ArcGIS Pro, set **Flight date** under Conditions.
  - The time zone defaults to your computer's, including daylight saving, or you can enter hours from UTC (e.g. 8 for Perth).
- **Daylight at the site** on that date: civil dawn, sunrise, solar noon, sunset and civil dusk.
- **Sun height for every flight** in the field schedule, shown in the report, the HTML brief and a new *Sun* column in the PDF.
- **Warnings**:
  - Flights outside civil twilight (*night*), which needs an approval in most places.
  - Flights in twilight, before sunrise or after sunset.
  - Flights with the sun below 30° (adjustable). Long shadows reduce mapping quality.
  - A time zone that doesn't fit the site.
- **Best-light window**: the time range when the sun is high enough for good mapping.
- The report and PDF show the flight date and the date prepared, and the automatic mission name uses the flight date.
- Uses a built-in solar calculator accurate to about a minute, so no internet is needed. Weather and NOTAMs still need checking before you fly.

## New in 1.11: airspace check

Checks whether the planned mission enters **controlled**, **restricted / prohibited**, **danger / military** or **uncontrolled** airspace.

- **Airspace sources:**
  - Any airspace layer in your project, such as national AIP shapefiles, FAA data or openAIP exports.
  - OpenAir files, the common worldwide airspace format.
  - GeoJSON files.
  - ED-269 files (EU drone zones).
  - Download from the FAA (USA, no key), including drone ceiling grids (UAS facility maps).
  - Download from openAIP (worldwide, needs a free API key).
- **Checked in 3D along every flight path:**
  - Heights come from the take-off elevation or DEM.
  - Airspace limits can be in feet or metres above sea level, above ground, or flight levels.
- **Each airspace is reported as:**
  - **INSIDE**: the mission enters it.
  - **Below floor**: the mission flies under its base, shown with the margin.
  - **Nearby**: within a distance you set.

  The report says what each case means, e.g. approval needed for controlled airspace.
- **Where results appear:**
  - Warnings in the panel report.
  - An **Airspace** layer (QGIS) or `_airspace` feature class (ArcGIS).
  - Dashed airspace outlines on the brief and PDF maps.
  - A new **Airspace** section in the HTML brief and PDF.

**Not an authoritative source.** Airspace data can be out of date and doesn't include NOTAMs or temporary restrictions. Always confirm with your authority's approved tools before flight: a CASA-verified app in Australia, B4UFLY or LAANC in the USA.

## Also new (1.10, first released here): flight date and light

- **Flight date, site time zone and arrival time.** In QGIS they're in **When and conditions**; in ArcGIS Pro, set **Flight date** under Conditions.
- **Daylight at the site** on that date: civil dawn, sunrise, solar noon, sunset and civil dusk.
- **Sun height for every flight**, shown in the report, the HTML brief and a new *Sun* column in the PDF.
- **Warnings** for flights at night (outside civil twilight), in twilight, or with the sun below 30° (adjustable). Long shadows reduce mapping quality.
- **Best-light window**: the time range when the sun is high enough for good mapping.
- The report and PDF show the flight date and the date prepared.
- Uses a built-in solar calculator, so no internet is needed.

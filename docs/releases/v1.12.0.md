# Drone Mission Planner v1.12.0

## Downloads

| File | For | Install |
|---|---|---|
| `drone_mission_planner_qgis.zip` | QGIS 3.22+ and QGIS 4 | *Plugins ▸ Manage and Install Plugins ▸ Install from ZIP* |
| `DroneMissionPlanner_ArcGISPro.zip` | ArcGIS Pro 2.9+ | Unzip, then *Catalog ▸ Toolboxes ▸ Add Toolbox* ▸ `DroneMissionPlanner.pyt` |

## New in 1.12

- **Automatic airspace for the project area** is now the default. When you plan, the airspace around the mission (plus 10 km) is downloaded and checked.
  - In the USA it comes from the FAA, with no key needed.
  - Everywhere else it comes from openAIP. Enter your free openAIP API key once; it's remembered.
  - The download is re-used while the mission stays in that area.
  - Without a key outside the USA, or with no internet, the report says the airspace was **not checked**.
- **Mission footprint**: the outline of all the ground the photos cover is always shown as a **yellow outline with no fill**, in QGIS and ArcGIS and on the report and PDF maps. Its area is listed as "Image coverage".
- **Individual photo footprints are optional.** Tick *Calculate individual photo footprints* to get them; it's **off by default** to keep large missions fast.
- **Take-off and launch stations are red dots** in QGIS and on the report and PDF maps.

## Notes

- Airspace data is a planning aid, not an authoritative source. Confirm with your authority's approved app and check NOTAMs before flight.
- In ArcGIS Pro the outputs use ArcGIS's default symbols. The yellow footprint outline and red dots are set in QGIS and on the report maps.
- Not yet tested against a licensed ArcGIS Pro or flown on real aircraft. Please report anything odd.

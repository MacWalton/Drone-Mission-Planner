# Drone Mission Planner v1.7.0

Plan drone mapping missions in **QGIS** or **ArcGIS Pro** from a drawn or imported (GIS/CAD) boundary or route line. Tune them for wind, temperature, altitude and payload, plan battery changes for big jobs, and send them to the aircraft.

## Downloads

| File | For | Install |
|---|---|---|
| `drone_mission_planner_qgis.zip` | QGIS 3.22+ and QGIS 4 | *Plugins ▸ Manage and Install Plugins ▸ Install from ZIP* |
| `DroneMissionPlanner_ArcGISPro.zip` | ArcGIS Pro 2.9+ | Unzip, then *Catalog ▸ Toolboxes ▸ Add Toolbox* ▸ `DroneMissionPlanner.pyt` |

Optional: `pymavlink` for live upload to ArduPilot/PX4 (see README).

## New in 1.7

- **DJI Mini Pro series**: profiles for the Mini 3 Pro, Mini 4 Pro and Mini 5 Pro, with standard and Plus batteries.
  - Mini 3 Pro and Mini 4 Pro: fly through Litchi.
  - Mini 4 Pro and Mini 5 Pro: fly with DJI Fly waypoints.
- **Photo trigger modes**:
  - *every N m*: distance trigger.
  - *camera timed shot*: for DJI Fly, which can't trigger by distance. The speed is matched to the camera interval.
  - *stop & shoot*: hover and take a photo at every photo point. The extra time is included in flight time and battery planning.
- **DJI Fly route import**: the export copies the aircraft codes from a route DJI Fly made on your controller. *Upload ▸ Replace a mission file made in DJI Fly / Pilot 2* overwrites that route and keeps a `.bak`. This is a community workaround, not supported by DJI.
- **Basemap under the field-brief maps**: the brief's maps now have a basemap for context:
  - *Current map*: in QGIS the visible layers; in ArcGIS Pro the active map's basemap, matched to the same online service.
  - OpenStreetMap, Esri World Imagery, Esri World Topographic, or None.
  - Attribution is printed on each map. If there's no internet, the brief is still written, with a plain map.

## Also included (1.0 – 1.6)

- Area (grid / crosshatch) and route / corridor mapping. Lines can be picked from a layer, with chainage (KP), KP windows and asymmetric widths.
- Choose the flying height or the GSD; set overlaps, heading, speed, gimbal angle and shutter; choose any projected CRS (automatic UTM by default).
- Flight-time model for wind, temperature, take-off elevation, battery reserve and health, and a **payload list** (weight and power per item).
- **Battery changes for big jobs**: mid-line resume, multiple launch stations (auto-placed or your own), VLOS range limit, leapfrog moves, and a field schedule with battery sets and chargers.
- **DEM**: auto-download (Copernicus GLO-30 / AWS Terrain Tiles), terrain following, terrain and height-limit checks, route elevation profile.
- **Minimum height and obstacles**: tree canopy, powerlines and buildings, with local climbs, a margin and a buffer.
- Return-to-home and battery-change heights in metres or feet.
- **Exports**: DJI WPML `.kmz` (Pilot 2 / DJI Fly), QGroundControl `.plan`, Mission Planner `.waypoints`, Litchi `.csv`, KML, GeoJSON, CSV, and a printable HTML **field brief** with mission and section maps.
- **Upload**: live over MAVLink (ArduPilot / PX4, sets the RTL altitude), or copied to the controller, SD card or USB stick.

## Known limits

- The ArcGIS Pro toolbox was tested against an `arcpy` stand-in, not a licensed ArcGIS Pro. Please report anything odd.
- DEM downloads, basemap tiles and DJI Fly imports were tested with simulated servers and files. They haven't yet been tried against the live services or real aircraft.
- Always check the mission in the flight app and fly within your local rules, e.g. the 120 m (400 ft) AGL limit.

description=Plan drone mapping missions (area grids and route corridors) from drawn polygons or GIS/CAD files, and send them to DJI, ArduPilot, PX4, Litchi and Autel aircraft.
about=Draw or import a boundary (Shapefile, GeoPackage, GeoJSON, KML, DXF/DWG CAD and any OGR format), choose the coordinate system, pick take-off and landing points, set flying height or ground pixel size (GSD), overlaps and heading, and get a mission tuned for wind, temperature, altitude and payload with automatic battery splitting. Exports DJI WPML KMZ (Pilot 2 / FlightHub 2), QGroundControl .plan, Mission Planner .waypoints, Litchi CSV, KML, GeoJSON and CSV, and uploads live to MAVLink autopilots. Optional terrain following from a DEM. Large jobs are split into battery-sized flights across multiple launch stations with a field schedule; linear features (roads, pipelines, powerlines, rail, rivers) can be picked on the map, chained and mapped by chainage.
